Privacy Policy — The King's Keep

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game The King's Keep (also known as Дом короля) handles information when you use the app. The developer is Varfacko.

1. Information handled by the game

The game does not require a user account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or payment-card details during normal gameplay. Game progress and settings are primarily stored locally on your device.

2. Advertising

The Android version of the game may use Google AdMob to display rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements. Rewarded ads are shown only when the player chooses to view them for an in-game benefit. Interstitial ads may be shown at appropriate transition points in the game.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, ad interactions, approximate location derived from network information, diagnostics, and other data needed to provide, measure, secure, and personalize advertising, subject to your consent choices and applicable law.

3. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, including for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the game may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent or present privacy choices before requesting certain advertising.

If a privacy-options control is required for your region, you can use it in the game to review or change your consent choices. You can also manage advertising settings through your Android/Google account and device settings.

4. Third-party services

The game may rely on the following third-party service for advertising and consent management:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK / Google UMP — Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

Third-party services process data under their own privacy policies and retention rules. The developer does not control Google's independent data-processing practices.

5. Local game data

Save data, progress, configuration, and similar gameplay information are stored locally on the device unless a future feature clearly tells you otherwise. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app may remove this local data.

6. Purchases and payments

If paid features or purchases are added in the future, payment processing will be handled by the applicable app-store payment system. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details from the store.

7. Children's privacy

The game is not designed to request personal contact information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, please contact the developer so the matter can be reviewed. Advertising behavior may also be limited according to app-store settings, consent status, and applicable law.

8. Data security

Reasonable technical measures are used to limit unnecessary collection by the game itself. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and third-party services are responsible for the security of data they process.

9. International processing

Google and its service providers may process data in countries other than the country where you live, subject to their legal safeguards and applicable privacy laws.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game, advertising setup, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.
